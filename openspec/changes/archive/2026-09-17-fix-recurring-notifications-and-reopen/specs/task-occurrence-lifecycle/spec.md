## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Recurring occurrence completion preserves occurrence state

Completing a recurring occurrence SHALL require a valid `occurrenceScheduledAt`, SHALL persist that occurrence as completed, and SHALL preserve any existing supported occurrence overrides. The recurring series definition SHALL remain uncompleted and unchanged by the occurrence completion.

#### Scenario: Complete a virtual occurrence
- **WHEN** a client completes a valid virtual recurring occurrence
- **THEN** the system SHALL persist that occurrence with completed status and a completion timestamp
- **AND** it SHALL leave the recurring series definition uncompleted

#### Scenario: Complete a modified occurrence
- **WHEN** a client completes a valid occurrence that has persisted content, priority, schedule, or deadline overrides
- **THEN** the system SHALL mark the occurrence completed while preserving every override

#### Scenario: Complete a recurring series without occurrence identity
- **WHEN** a client attempts to complete a recurring series without `occurrenceScheduledAt`
- **THEN** the system SHALL reject the request
- **AND** it SHALL not mark the series definition or any occurrence completed

#### Scenario: Complete an unavailable occurrence
- **WHEN** a client attempts to complete an occurrence that is already completed, skipped, or not generated by the series recurrence rule
- **THEN** the system SHALL reject the request without changing the series or occurrence state

### Requirement: Reopening restores the occurrence's prior open representation

Reopening a recurring occurrence SHALL require a valid `occurrenceScheduledAt` and an existing completed occurrence state. The operation SHALL clear completion without discarding supported occurrence overrides. If no overrides remain, the operation SHALL restore the occurrence to its virtual state.

#### Scenario: Reopen a completed modified occurrence
- **WHEN** a completed recurring occurrence contains one or more supported overrides
- **THEN** reopening SHALL clear its completion timestamp and restore modified status
- **AND** it SHALL preserve every occurrence override

#### Scenario: Reopen a completed unmodified occurrence
- **WHEN** a completed recurring occurrence contains no supported override
- **THEN** reopening SHALL remove its persisted occurrence state
- **AND** the returned occurrence SHALL be open and virtual

#### Scenario: Reopen a recurring series without occurrence identity
- **WHEN** a client attempts to reopen a recurring series without `occurrenceScheduledAt`
- **THEN** the system SHALL reject the request
- **AND** it SHALL not mutate completion fields on the series definition

#### Scenario: Reopen an occurrence that is not completed
- **WHEN** a client attempts to reopen a virtual, modified, or skipped occurrence that is not completed
- **THEN** the system SHALL reject the request without changing its state

### Requirement: Non-recurring completion behavior is preserved

Completing or reopening a non-recurring task SHALL retain the existing task-level completion behavior and SHALL NOT create recurring-occurrence state.

#### Scenario: Complete and reopen a non-recurring task
- **WHEN** a client completes and subsequently reopens a non-recurring task
- **THEN** the system SHALL set and then clear the task's completion fields according to the existing behavior
- **AND** it SHALL not create a recurring occurrence record
