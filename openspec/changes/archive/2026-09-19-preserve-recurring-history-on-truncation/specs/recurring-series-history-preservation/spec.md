## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Truncation preserves meaningful stranded occurrence state

Every operation that truncates a recurring series SHALL classify occurrence state whose stable anchor is at or after the first instant no longer generated by the original series. Completed and modified state SHALL remain associated with the original series and SHALL be marked detached. Skipped state SHALL be removed.

#### Scenario: Truncation strands a completed occurrence
- **WHEN** a recurring series is truncated before an occurrence with completed state
- **THEN** the system SHALL retain that state and mark it detached
- **AND** the completed fact SHALL remain associated with the original series

#### Scenario: Truncation strands a modified occurrence
- **WHEN** a recurring series is truncated before an occurrence with modified state
- **THEN** the system SHALL retain that state and mark it detached
- **AND** it SHALL preserve the occurrence's supported overrides

#### Scenario: Truncation strands a skipped occurrence
- **WHEN** a recurring series is truncated before an occurrence with skipped state
- **THEN** the system SHALL delete that state because no generated occurrence remains to skip

#### Scenario: Any truncation path processes stranded state
- **WHEN** the series is truncated by partial following update, complete following replacement, or following deletion
- **THEN** the system SHALL apply the same detached-state classification from the cut identity onward

### Requirement: Detached occurrences remain independently queryable

A calendar date or date-range query SHALL include detached completed and modified occurrence state according to its effective scheduled date even when the backing series is not active in that period. Detached state SHALL be materialized as a recurring occurrence and SHALL NOT depend on current recurrence expansion for discovery.

#### Scenario: Detached occurrence falls inside the requested period
- **WHEN** a detached occurrence's effective schedule falls within a requested calendar period
- **THEN** the system SHALL include it as a materialized recurring occurrence
- **AND** it SHALL retain its association with the original series

#### Scenario: Backing series is inactive in the requested period
- **WHEN** detached state falls inside the requested period but its series ended before that period
- **THEN** the system SHALL still include the detached occurrence

#### Scenario: Attached moved state lies outside the series span
- **WHEN** non-detached moved state has an anchor outside the current span of its series
- **THEN** the system SHALL NOT display it through the moved-occurrence overlay path

### Requirement: The successor schedule is independent from the cut identity

A following-series mutation SHALL use the targeted occurrence identity to end the original series. When the mutation supplies a replacement `scheduledAt`, the successor series SHALL use that value as its DTSTART; otherwise a partial update SHALL fall back to the cut identity.

#### Scenario: Following update moves the successor rhythm
- **WHEN** a partial following-series update targets one occurrence and supplies a different `scheduledAt`
- **THEN** the original series SHALL end before the targeted occurrence
- **AND** the successor SHALL begin from the supplied schedule

#### Scenario: Following update omits the successor schedule
- **WHEN** a partial following-series update does not supply `scheduledAt`
- **THEN** the successor SHALL use the targeted occurrence identity as its schedule
