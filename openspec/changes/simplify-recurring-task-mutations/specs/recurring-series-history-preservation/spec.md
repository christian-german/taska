## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Truncation preserves meaningful stranded occurrence state
Stopping a series SHALL classify states whose stable anchors are at or after the cut. Completed and modified states SHALL remain associated with the original series and become detached; skipped states there SHALL be removed. Stopping SHALL create no successor. The cut SHALL be an occurrence still generated by the series and SHALL NOT extend a previous series end.

#### Scenario: Stop strands moved or completed state
- **WHEN** a series is stopped before a moved or completed occurrence's original identity
- **THEN** that state SHALL survive as detached and retain its identity and schedule

#### Scenario: Stop strands skipped state
- **WHEN** a series is stopped before a skipped occurrence's identity
- **THEN** its skipped state SHALL be removed because the generator no longer produces it

## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: The successor schedule is independent from the cut identity
**Reason**: Following-series replacement and successor creation are removed.
**Migration**: Stop the original series; no replacement is created by that operation.
