## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Base task updates replace the complete mutable task representation
PUT `/tasks/{taskId}` SHALL require the complete typed TaskUpdateRequest: content, type, description, projectId, parentId, order, priority, labels, scheduledAt, dueAt, allDay, isRecurring, estimateMinutes, mentionContext and recurrenceRule. Unknown, retired and output-only properties SHALL be rejected. Existing recurring series SHALL reject changes to recurring status, normalized recurrence rule, scheduledAt or allDay even when no occurrence state exists. Recurring definitions SHALL have null dueAt and non-null scheduledAt. Other supported values SHALL retain full-replacement semantics and responses SHALL retain their discriminated resource types.

#### Scenario: Reject a generator edit on a stateless series
- **WHEN** replacement changes an existing series schedule, recurrence rule, recurring status or all-day classification
- **THEN** the system SHALL reject it without changing the series or occurrence history

#### Scenario: Replace a non-recurring task
- **WHEN** a valid complete request replaces a non-recurring task
- **THEN** every supported mutable property SHALL take the supplied value

#### Scenario: Reject incomplete replacement
- **WHEN** a required property is omitted or an output-only property is supplied
- **THEN** the request SHALL fail without changing the resource

### Requirement: Single-occurrence updates use an occurrence-specific replacement resource
PUT `/tasks/{taskId}/occurrences/{occurrenceScheduledAt}` SHALL accept only a required nullable scheduledAt in OccurrenceUpdateRequest. A non-null value moves the occurrence; null restores its original schedule. The operation SHALL preserve completion, original identity and detached classification. The target MUST be generated by the current series or identify existing detached state. The response SHALL remain RecurringTaskOccurrenceDto. Unsupported properties SHALL be rejected without mutation.

#### Scenario: Move an occurrence
- **WHEN** a valid schedule-only replacement targets an occurrence
- **THEN** only its effective schedule SHALL change and other occurrences SHALL remain unchanged

#### Scenario: Reset a detached occurrence schedule
- **WHEN** scheduledAt is null for existing detached state
- **THEN** the effective schedule SHALL become its original occurrenceScheduledAt while the state remains detached

#### Scenario: Reject a retired occurrence property
- **WHEN** title, priority, dueAt or any other unsupported property is supplied
- **THEN** the request SHALL be rejected before mutation

### Requirement: Partial base mutations preserve recurring history
Unscoped partial mutations SHALL enforce the same immutable generator rule as complete replacement regardless of persisted occurrence state. Scoped occurrence partial mutations SHALL accept only scheduling with THIS_ONLY and a stable identity. FROM_THIS updates SHALL be rejected.

#### Scenario: MCP partial update changes a stateless series rule
- **WHEN** a partial mutation changes the rule of an existing series with no occurrence state
- **THEN** it SHALL fail before any persistent mutation

### Requirement: Task mutations preserve the recurring schedule invariant
All complete or partial base mutations SHALL validate resulting recurring status and scheduledAt before persistence. Existing series SHALL not be converted to non-recurring tasks or have their anchor removed. Non-recurring tasks retain their existing scheduling behavior.

#### Scenario: Remove a recurring schedule
- **WHEN** replacement removes the schedule of an existing series
- **THEN** it SHALL fail without persistent changes

#### Scenario: Remove a non-recurring schedule
- **WHEN** valid replacement removes a non-recurring task's schedule
- **THEN** it SHALL persist scheduledAt as null

## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: Following-series updates split and replace a complete series
**Reason**: Series splitting and successor creation are no longer supported.
**Migration**: Remove the following PUT endpoint and its request schema; use series stopping when the recurrence must end.
